# Privacy Policy — Vexario AI Agent

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

Vexario AI Agent is an internal content automation tool used to manage and publish content for Vexario.

## Information We Access

The application may access information required to perform its automation functions, including:

- Pinterest account information necessary for publishing Pins.
- Product images, descriptions, prices, and other product information provided by Vexario.
- Content and credentials required for authorized API integrations.

## How We Use Information

Information accessed by the application is used only to:

- Create and publish Vexario content.
- Manage product-related content.
- Automate approved social-media publishing workflows.
- Maintain and operate the Vexario content automation system.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Data Security

Access to connected services is controlled through authorized API credentials and access tokens. Credentials are stored separately from the public source code and are not intentionally published.

## Third-Party Services

Vexario AI Agent may use third-party APIs and services, including Pinterest and Telegram, to perform authorized publishing and automation functions. Their respective privacy policies and terms may also apply.

## Data Retention

The application does not intentionally retain personal information beyond what is necessary to operate its authorized automation functions.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or integrations change.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Vexario AI Agent, contact the Vexario team through the contact method associated with the Vexario brand.
